CORE MODULES

Everything a home runs on, without the noise.

Five focused workflows built for shared family routines—fast offline data entry on every phone, converging automatically when your household is on home Wi-Fi.

Shared Todos

Household chores, maintenance tasks, and daily reminders with one-time or recurring routines, Today surfacing, and timely local notifications.

Grouped Shopping Lists

Smart quantity and item-type detection, store-by-store grouping across online apps and local shops, plus one-tap checklist sharing.

Household Expenses (₹)

Fast cash, UPI, and card logging stored in exact integer paise—with custom or equal family splits, monthly category budgets, and recurring bills.

Meal Planning

Plan weekly breakfasts, lunches, and dinners so daily cooking stays calm. Manage a household recipe library with scalable servings and push missing ingredients straight onto your shopping list in a single tap.

Staff Attendance & Payroll

Log daily check-ins, check-outs, and leaves for house help. Calculate salaries cleanly from effective-dated monthly or hourly pay rules, advances, and bonuses, then settle transparent pay slips linked directly to expenses.

WHY ILLAM?

Private by architecture, not just by promise.

Your family’s daily life belongs on your devices—not in someone else’s database. Illam is engineered to work completely offline with verifiable cryptography.

100% Local-First

Your household data lives in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLite3MultipleCiphers) sealed with hardware-backed keys in Android Keystore. Every screen works instantaneously in airplane mode.

Peer-to-Peer Wi-Fi Sync

Pair family phones over your home Wi-Fi using a QR code or passphrase. Devices reconcile edits automatically via deterministic CRDT / Hybrid Logical Clock merge rules over end-to-end encrypted channels.

Portable Encrypted Backups

Create scheduled passphrase-encrypted .nmbak snapshots stored where you decide: phone/USB folders, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, pCloud, Nextcloud, WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, or SFTP.

Zero Bloat & Open Source

No user accounts, no telemetry or ad SDKs, no subscriptions, and Android allowBackup="false" enforced by default. Auditable source code licensed under Apache 2.0.

Flutter · Material 3 Drift + SQLite3MultipleCiphers XChaCha20 · Ed25519 · SPAKE2 mDNS Local Discovery
