Illam (இல்லம் — "home") keeps everyday household routines organised without accounts, subscriptions or servers. All data lives on your family's phones and syncs directly between them over your home Wi-Fi.

<b>Features</b>
• Shared todos and recurring routines with local reminders
• Grouped shopping lists with store mode
• Household expenses, monthly budgets and recurring bills
• Weekly meal planner and recipes that add missing ingredients to your list
• House-help attendance, leave and monthly salary with pay slips

<b>Private by design</b>
• Fully offline; encrypted local database
• Peer-to-peer end-to-end encrypted Wi-Fi sync between family phones
• Password-encrypted backups to a place you choose: folder, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, pCloud, Nextcloud/WebDAV, S3 or SFTP
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking

<b>Experimental, off by default</b>
• Remote sync through your own Firebase project
• Gmail invoice import with your own Google OAuth client
• SMS spend import: payment SMS are parsed on the phone into drafts you approve

Android permissions such as camera (QR pairing) and SMS (optional import) are only used for the features you switch on.
